THE EXECUTION PIPELINE

Every signal has to earn its way to an order.

Sacred Soul treats a message, a risk decision, and an execution as three distinct events. That creates better control, clearer audit trails, and a safer way to test a source before enabling automation.

01

Connect a source

Use the Discord channel picker or a supported inbound route. Select specific channels, not an entire account.

02

Normalize the instruction

Direction, instrument, entry, stops, targets and updates become visible structured data—or the message is rejected.

03

Enforce your guardrails

Position size, daily loss, symbol rules, duplicate orders and account restrictions are evaluated before routing.

04

Review the outcome

Track every accepted, rejected, queued and filled action from the same operations dashboard.
